## Root cause and preservation

Legacy026 building and050 unit authoring scripts wrote tangent values into
offset 12 (UV0). Declared native layouts use true tangent slots at 20 for structure
and 16 for unit streams. Earlier handoff recovery repaired many groups but left
these 22 files partially damaged. The 22 retained originals provide exact indexed
UV correspondence even when UV-signature-based discovery was inconclusive.

091 changes UV0 bytes 12–15 per vertex only. All position, normal, actual tangent,
secondary UV, collection selector, skin/weight, index, rig and other chunk bytes
are preserved from090. No custom models are replaced with donor geometry.
Do not rerun legacy026/050 geometry generation into a release without applying
the091 repair and byte checks; historical recipes remain unchanged to preserve
their recorded source/rebuild hashes. This recipe is the authoritative UV fix.

Native import, appearance, colours, construction/destruction, actions, voices,
multiplayer and gameplay acceptance remain separate player checks.
