# Berber Dynasty music catalog

48 original instrumental recordings;three theme families per age,each arranged
for Peace,Skirmish,War andMassiveBattle. Playback follows the local player age
and combat automatically; themes rotate during peace.

| Age | Theme A | Theme B | Theme C |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dark Age | Atlas Dawn | Caravan of the Dunes | Embers of the Ancestors |
| Feudal Age | Cedar and Stone | Roads of Salt and Gold | Oaths Beneath the Stars |
| Castle Age | Gardens of Marrakech | Riders of the Trade Roads | Citadels of the Atlas |
| Imperial Age | Court of the Golden Horizon | Banners Across the Sahara | Legacy of the Mountain Kings |

Original recordings remain editable outside the mod. Every gameplay track has
three alternate entry points at10seconds,25percent and40percent of its original
timeline,with the initial10seconds removed. Only the firstDarkAge AtlasPeace
opening includes them. Source keys/BPM reflect composition requests.
