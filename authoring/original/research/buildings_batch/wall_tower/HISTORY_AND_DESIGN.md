# Maghrebi stone wall tower source

UNESCO's Rabat nomination describes 12th-century Almohad defensive lime-rich clay-earth concrete ramparts punctuated by square towers. This supports the battered square mass, warm earth/lime surfaces and restrained crenellation. Sources: https://whc.unesco.org/document/152576 and https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1401 .

Elevated through walk, emplacement machinery, exact age progression, neutral owner band and simplified chamber/stair dimensions are game-design interpretations, not a monument reconstruction. No mosque/minaret silhouette or modern emblem was used. Original geometry materially changes the architectural mass while remaining within the native render envelope and matching the wall deck.

Untouched decoded Malian geometry is kept in its own reference blend; native RGM/RGO/RRGeometry/material inputs remain in the research stock directory. Garrison reports no skeleton in this structure collection. The stamp attachment points to common layout_stonewall_tower. Layout decode could not resolve a mesh provider; no rig was fabricated.
