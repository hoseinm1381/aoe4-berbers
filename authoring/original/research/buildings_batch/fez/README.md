# Madrasa of Fez — source delivery

Age IV completed landmark, built during Age III. See AGE_AND_TARGET_EVIDENCE.json. Do not infer age from filename. Current nested Fez XML uses 6×6 actual site footprint and 130% animator scale. Authored ground envelope 4.4×4.4 gives 5.72×5.72 at that scale. Keep centered ground origin. Do not multiply source geometry by 1.3 and also retain animator_scale130. Original design: two upper student-cell wings over six hollow arcades, guest accommodation by the portal, rear teaching bay, carved cedar beams, geometric plaster relief and ceramic dado, a hollow fountain basin with water, and a teaching desk/manuscript. Architecture is an interpretive Marinid college, not a surveyed replica. Its compact vertical cells and highly fitted court distinguish it from the broader single-storey University and Mosque. The hollow arch module is validated before repetition; each final mesh is independent.
