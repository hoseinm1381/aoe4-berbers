# Outpost source family I–IV

Source-authoring delivery only. Live minimum age I and official 4×4m footprint are separately verified in AGE_AND_TARGET_EVIDENCE.json. Official upgrades unlock fortified stone and arrow slits in II, springald in III, cannon in IV. Each age blend includes separate state collections; state manifests and named FBXs cover base, fortified, exclusive emplacements and fortified-emplacement combinations. A later-age base tower is not automatically fortified. Existing gameplay XML, damage, range, garrison and research semantics were untouched.

Architecture research: https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444 (UNESCO Ksar of Ait-Ben-Haddou) documents earthen construction and defensive corner towers. UNESCO dates the surviving examples largely to the 17th century; this supports material/vernacular vocabulary, not a precise medieval reconstruction. The simple earth tower, square lookout, later lime/corner stone, restrained geometric plaster, cedar door and roof are design inferences adapted for a medieval game family. No inscriptions or modern emblems are used.

Installed Garrison 0.1.4 catalog selected Abbasid outpost control age01: the current Malian target itself uses the Abbasid attachment list. Stock archive bytes and decoded geometry are retained under stock; Untouched_Abbasid_Outpost_Age01_Reference.blend preserves mesh positions and topology without reconstructed materials. Donor render bounds are recorded separately, not treated as gameplay footprint. New geometry allows auditable real firing cuts, explicit roof contact and visible age progression. No stock meshes are modified.

Anatomy: centered 3.6m foundation; 3.0m square hollow shaft with .38m walls; door threshold; fitted 3.16m lookout floor/parapet; four corner merlons; four roof posts with a rear half canopy. Base roof floor elevations rise from 4.31m in I to 4.73m in IV. A central 1m front embrasure gives siege weapons clear fire; source alternatives are fitted within the 4m footprint. Neutral owner stripe is split on the front parapet and continuous on one side. Owner pattern is red-only, shares the albedo UV, and materials carry ber_owner_mask. Native colour route is blocked per the shared PLAYER_COLOUR_CONTRACT.md.

fx_muz_arrow_00..03, fx_muz_springald_00..03 and fx_muz_cannon_00..03 preserve the runtime XML names. Empty axes propose cardinal source muzzle positions; front muzzle is -Y and marker00, with remaining markers rotated around Z. These are new source coordinates, not untouched donor/native anchor transforms. Front springald marker seats at the loaded bolt tip; cannon marker at barrel mouth center. Actual front geometry is fixed in the source; native tracking/rotation, attachment selectors and cardinal shot mapping remain qualification gates. Do not substitute these FBXs into production before that mapping is verified.

The ground door is recessed into an actual cut, with an inserted closed cedar door. Its construction is source geometry, not a native open-door animation or navigation proof. Slits have real through-cuts; source ray tests establish clearance through side walls. Canopy posts sit on roof floor, canopy underside meets post tops, and beams are seated at the same plane. Construction manifests explicitly list first-visible percentages and state meshes; they do not activate native selectors automatically.

Reproduce with run_hidden.py using bundled Python; build_outpost.py contains the final recipe. create_recipe.py retains initial generation, repair_embrasure.py retains the interface correction. The runner owns a hidden Blender process, private config, PID and logs. No Garrison cook or mod archive generation was requested. Final gates: native structure owner shader; attachment/weapon rotation and FX; construction and upgrade selectors; LOD/damage/snow/burning; scale/orientation and two-owner solo match.
