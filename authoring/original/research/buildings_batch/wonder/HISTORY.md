# Almohad great sanctuary — Age IV interpretation

Research checked 2026-10-13. UNESCO's [Rabat dossier](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1401/) identifies Hassan Mosque and the Almohad capital project as twelfth-century heritage. Its [ICOMOS evaluation](https://whc.unesco.org/document/152576) explicitly describes the incomplete mosque and surviving minaret, flagged floor, column bases and walls. Morocco's [Ministry of Habous account](https://www.habous.gov.ma/fr/atlas-des-mosquees/93-la-mosquee-hassan-de-rabat.html) records the present tower as 44 m and discusses an intended taller proportion. These sources differ on the initial year (1184 versus 1196); this asset does not settle that chronology.

MIT's [Great Mosque of Tinmal collection](https://dome.mit.edu/handle/1721.3/50281) identifies the Almohad/hypostyle tradition. The Ministry's [Moroccan mosque spatial survey](https://habous.gov.ma/fr/component/content/article/244-architecture-des-mosqu%C3%A9es/1043-aper%C3%A7u-sur-l-organisation-spatiale-des-mosqu%C3%A9es-marocaines.html) provides the prayer hall/nave and courtyard context. These are architectural evidence; the compact game-scale arrangement below is inference.

Original imagined completed Almohad sanctuary: broad rear hypostyle hall with repeating hollow horseshoe arcade, raised central nave, two courtyard galleries, central tower gateway, square sandstone minaret shaft and open square lantern, restrained sebka-like geometric relief, coursed lime/ochre masonry, cedar roof drains and large neutral owner banners. It is not a reconstruction of completed Hassan Mosque: the historical monument was unfinished, and this coherent completed composition is explicitly invented for a Berber endgame Wonder. No Ottoman dome, modern national emblem or pseudo-calligraphy.

Anatomy/proportions: native site 12×12; platform 11.6×11.6; prayer hall 10.9×4.4, wall height 2.65; courtyard clear region ~7.0×4.3; tower width 2.2 versus total height ~8.3; galleries 1.7 wide. All sit around origin (0,0,0); visual footprint fit is checked independently of the XML footprint.

It is a Wonder, not an age-up landmark: construction and completion are both Imperial.
