# Barracks source family — 2026-10-13

Original Berber fortified military training court. Age I: earthen armoury/store, open drilled court, timber pitched weapon shelter, spear racks, round shields, drill dummies and supply crates. II adds buttresses and roof parapets. III develops limework and crenellations. IV adds restrained geometric gateway relief and plaster detail. The progression is art direction rather than a dated reconstruction.

History: [UNESCO Rabat](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/1401/) identifies surviving 12th-century Almohad ramparts and gates: medieval fortification and gate cues. [UNESCO Ait-Ben-Haddou](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/444/) documents earthen wall/tower ensembles, wood and geometric upper wall motifs. Its oldest existing buildings are 17th century; the older technique continuity is evidence, not a medieval barracks plan. Court drill equipment, weapon storage and age progression are design inference. Neither source is evidence for a specific Berber barracks layout.
