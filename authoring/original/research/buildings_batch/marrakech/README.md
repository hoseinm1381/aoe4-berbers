# Dar al-Jihad of Marrakech source handoff

Dar al-Jihad of Marrakech is the mod's game-invented landmark name. This is an original fortified administrative and training palace, not a reconstruction of a surviving building by that name. The actual historical reference is Almohad Marrakech's royal kasbah and its monumental Bab Agnaou entrance. No exact medieval palace plan, training-target layout or age-IV rebuilding history is asserted.

Evidence: the Marrakech-Safi Regional Tourism Council's official route describes the kasbah as an Almohad royal residential/palatial area with open urban spaces, Bab Agnaou as a decorated cut-stone entrance, and the city's ochre rammed-earth defensive walls. https://www.visitmarrakech.com/en/listing/thousand-and-one-doors/ (read 2026-10-13). The JCIC Heritage FY2025 Morocco survey search excerpt identifies Bab Agnaou as an 1188/1190 Almohad western entrance and the kasbah as a palace city housing royal-court people: https://www.jcic-heritage.jp/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/FY2025_Survey_Morocco_EN.pdf . The full large PDF could not be opened by the browser; this latter evidence is excerpt-only and is not the sole design authority.

Inference/authoring: monumental horseshoe portal with carved geometric alfiz, ochre walls, clear court, connected rampart walks, hollow rear office arcade, chest, provision sacks under fitted cloth shade and visible training targets. Low enclosing walls and administrative court distinguish it from the ordinary Keep and the grand keep landmark; there is no central keep tower or minaret. Age IV adds fitted cedar register, limestone repair/quoin panels and gate caps, preserving the footprint and role. These are fictional gameplay-age development choices.

Fresh installed parent/controller RGD and XML are preserved in this directory. Current mod target inherits core/building_wonder_age2 requirement rules: Feudal age present, Castle age absent, and its construction action is landmark_age_2_action_apply. The villager's mal_age2_wonders construction list references this exact target at unit_villager_1_mal.xml:1323. Thus construction is II, completion/target III, and completed authored variants are III-IV. construction_age2.json explicitly reuses Age III cumulative source stages. Native selectors/bindings remain pending.

Both live target site_ext and fresh official controller have 8x8 placement footprint. Render bounds are not used to infer gameplay footprint. No current animator_scale override exists: core and official controller use 100. Source geometry is measured within XY +/-4, ground Z=0, centered source origin. Native imported FBX orientation, attachment origin, emergence/hit anchors and stock construction-stamp replacement must be qualified before integration.

Closest actual inherited stock donor: Malian landmark_2b Farimba Garrison, official mal_landmark_b_02 attachment. Garrison 0.1.4 decoded it read-only with archive provenance in stock/. Untouched positions/topology are preserved in UNTouched_Malian_Landmark2b_Reference.blend; donor materials are not reconstructed. Original geometry fits the Marrakech kasbah brief better; no stock vertices are reused.

Five packed source maps are delivered: albedo, red-only pattern, flat normal, uniform roughness and zero metal. The latter three intentionally state untextured low-poly material values. Portal bands and provision-cloth canopy are neutral and ber_owner_mask=1; geometry samples are checked against the same-UV pattern. Native owner colour is blocked by the shared player-colour contract, not completed by these inputs. Stages 0/20/40/60/80/100 are cumulative object authoring manifests, not native exported stage playback.

Rebuild with bundled general Python: prepare.py, run_build.py; then run_build.py audit_saved.py; then finalize.py. The build recipe uses copied task-local geometry helpers and launches task-owned hidden offline GUI Blender. No shared Blender edit, production XML edit, cook or distributable build is performed. Native structure shader, two-player colours, construction/age selectors, LOD, damage/destruction, scale/orientation/anchors, solo appearance and walk-surface behavior remain separate future gates.
