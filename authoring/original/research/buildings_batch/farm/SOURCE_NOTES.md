# Irrigated farm source family

Official installed Attrib.sga Malian farm is preserved as RGD and decoded XML here. `requirement_ext/requirement_table` is empty and `requirement_statetree` blank (830–834). Live Berber villager SBPS XML construction item at 125–137 targets this farm, uses age_1/build_farm, and has empty additional requirements. Authoring covers I–IV; the four-age appearance progression is a game art direction choice rather than a historical sequence or four native stock farm tiers.

Measured gameplay envelope comes from official `site_ext/footprint_scale` x/y=4 (103–106), forced axis-aligned footprint. The source stays within 3.8×3.8 with origin centered at ground. Resource spline layout box is separately 8×8 (423–424), desired rows=3, distance=2–2.3, four subsplines/row. These values are engine layout parameters, not render bounds. `impass_simboxes/default_simbox=false` is preserved as evidence; this source must not become a solid impassable building. Low edging leaves a wide south entry and access channels, and the tool canopy is less than 1m high.

Stock source: the official crop-resource animator is `farm_wheat_a`; its RGO and farm_wheat RGO could not decode through Garrison's same-stem static lane because their RRGeometry providers are absent. The closest available stock depleted crop mesh `art/environment/nat_economy/farm/farm_wheat/field_crop_depleted.rgm` decoded successfully from ArtHigh/DX12Assets. It is preserved unchanged as `UNTOUCHED_Stock_Depleted_Farm.blend`, with original decoded geometry/source bytes and SHA evidence under stock/. Its tiny crop-component bounds do not define the farm footprint. Final crops, beds, channels, shade and props are original geometry.

Credible design evidence: [FAO Atlas Mountains oasis system](https://www.fao.org/giahs/giahs-around-the-world/morocco-cold-oases-system/en) records integrated agricultural systems and gravity-fed khettara irrigation. [FAO Figuig](https://www.fao.org/giahs/giahs-around-the-world/morocco-ksour-figuig-oasis/en) describes irrigation networks extending to hundreds of oasis plots. [FAO Drâa-Tafilalet](https://www.fao.org/neareast/news/stories/details/revitalizing-the-dr%C3%A2a-tafilalet-oasis-agroecosystems/) identifies traditional open seguias channels with centuries of use. Evidence supports irrigated plot identity and channels; exact 3-bed arrangement, restrained low walls, tool shade and age upgrades are authored inference. These current heritage reports are not exact medieval reconstruction sources. No modern pumps or national emblems are used.

Anatomy: flat earth platform; three split raised cereal beds with a crossing and connected stem/ear topology; main header plus branches; low earthen/stone edges; ground-seated four-post tool shade, rack and hoes. I earth banks/plain shade; II sluice board/trough; III lime channel ends and fitted crossing; IV small header inlays/spare sluice board. Crop maturity/colour is illustrative source appearance, not a claim of native growth playback.

Source maps share a material-tile atlas; all polygon UVs have positive area. Same-material UV overlap is intentional repeating palette UVs, not a lightmap unwrap. Source normal is flat tangent RGB, roughness .87 and metal black. Meaningful shade cloth and edge strip are neutral with `ber_owner_mask=1`; all other material zones are 0. Red-only pattern shares exact atlas coordinates with albedo.

Native owner colour remains mandatory and unfinished: shared PLAYER_COLOUR_CONTRACT documents that static object export omits pattern and the attempted unit shader static lane failed. Masks here are authored inputs only. No native-ready or native-colour claim is made. Qualification still requires supported structure/attachment export, a bounded cook/readback, two distinct owners, stages, ages and LODs.

Explicit construction manifests assign cumulative first-visible 0/20/40/60/80/100 percentages. Final FBX export does not implement construction selectors. Each packed age file includes hidden source collections for untouched sprouts and depleted stubble, with the default mature gatherable crops visible. Separate whole-farm FBX state alternatives and per-age state manifests are provided. They are static source alternatives, not qualified growing/gathered animation; those variables require downstream bindings and preserved official XML supplies their names. Regrowth/gather harvesting, fire/death, walking and placement, native scale, destruction, LOD and native owner colour are pending. No production XML, GUID or archive was edited and no mod was cooked/built.

Reproduce using run_build.py with bundled Python, followed by run_build.py audit_states.py, then make_sheets.py. The launcher uses a private hidden GUI Blender 5.2, task-specific config, offline startup, captured log/PID and closes only its own Blender.
