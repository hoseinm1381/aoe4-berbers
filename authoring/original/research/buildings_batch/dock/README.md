Historical sources: [UNESCO Medina of Sousse](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/498/) supports an early medieval Maghrebi town organised toward its ancient inner port and regional Mediterranean building context. It does not document this dock plan or wooden pier details. [UNESCO Medina of Essaouira](https://whc.unesco.org/en/list/753/) supports Morocco's commercial harbour identity, but dates its planned fortified city to the late eighteenth century: it is a later comparative reference, not a medieval reconstruction. Ochre earth, limewashed stone, restrained horseshoe storage portals, working cargo, timber berth structure and neutral woven awnings are original gameplay-scale design inference.
